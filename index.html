FAQ

Quick answers to the things players ask most.

Is Bolt to Home free to play?

Yes. The full set of 200 levels is free. We support development through optional rewarded ads — watch one to grant yourself extra time, free Clear Box uses, or bonus gems. You never have to.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Every level, every gem, every star, every saved high score works completely offline. Ads only show when you're connected, and they never block your progress.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

Never. Your progress is stored on your device and stays on your device.

What devices does it run on?

Bolt to Home runs on Android 8.0 or higher and recent iOS. Both phones and tablets are supported in portrait mode.

How is data handled?

The game itself doesn't collect personal information. Our advertising SDK collects device identifiers and approximate (IP-based) location to serve relevant ads. Full details in the privacy policy.

Can I play without ads?

You can play through every level without ever watching an ad — ads are entirely opt-in for bonuses, and mandatory interstitials are limited with a 60-second floor between shows.

Is there a way to contact you?

Email brevabeestudios@gmail.com, or visit the support page for help and FAQs.
