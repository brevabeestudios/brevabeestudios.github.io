How to play

No tutorials, no jargon. Three steps from menu to victory.

1

Pick a level

Pick the next level on the title screen, or revisit any cleared level for a 3-star run.

2

Rotate the pipes

Tap a tile to rotate it 90°. Build a clean, connected path from the bolt to every home on the board.

3

Bring it home

Beat the timer to clear the level. Earn gems, climb the high score, unlock the next puzzle.

FAQ

Quick answers to the things players ask most.

Is Bolt to Home free to play?

Yes. The full set of 200 levels is free. We support development through optional rewarded ads — watch one to grant yourself extra time, free Clear Box uses, or bonus gems. You never have to.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Every level, every gem, every star, every saved high score works completely offline. Ads only show when you're connected, and they never block your progress.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

Never. Your progress is stored on your device and stays on your device.

What devices does it run on?

Bolt to Home runs on Android 6.0 (API 23) and iOS 13 or higher. Both phones and tablets are supported in portrait mode.

How is data handled?

The Game itself doesn't collect personal information. Our advertising SDK collects device identifiers and approximate (IP-based) location to serve relevant ads. Full details in the privacy policy.

Can I play without ads?

You can play through every level without ever watching an ad — ads are entirely opt-in for bonuses. Mandatory interstitials are limited to once every three "clean" clears (and never before level 11), with a 60-second floor between shows.

Is there a way to contact you?

Email brevabeestudios@gmail.com for feedback, support, or business inquiries.
