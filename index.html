Bolt to Home

What's inside

A focused puzzle experience designed to be picked up in seconds and replayed for stars.

🧩

200 levels

Progressive grid sizes from 3×3 up to 8×10, with shuffle aggressiveness scaled per band so the difficulty never plateaus.

⚡

Power-ups

Clear Box removes blockers; the 4-Way Tile upgrades any cell into a super-connector. Use them sparingly — every star counts.

⏱️

Beat the clock

Each puzzle has a tight timer with smart color escalation as it approaches zero. Run out of time? Earn gems for a +60s continue.

💎

Gem economy

Earn gems for every level cleared, every star, every clean run. Spend them on power-ups whenever the level demands it.

🎨

Evolving palette

Five tier-based color schemes that change as you progress — cyan, neon pink, emerald, inferno, and cosmic violet. Your milestone, your look.

📶

Plays offline

Every level, every gem, every star works offline. Ads are a bonus when connected — never a blocker when you're not.

How to play

No tutorials, no jargon. Just three steps from menu to victory.

1

Pick a level

Pick the next level on the title screen, or revisit any cleared level for a 3-star run.

2

Rotate the pipes

Tap a tile to rotate it 90°. Build a clean, connected path from the bolt to every home on the board.

3

Bring it home

Beat the timer to clear the level. Earn gems, climb the high score, unlock the next puzzle.
