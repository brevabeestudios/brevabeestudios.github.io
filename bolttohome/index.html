How to play

No tutorials, no jargon — three steps from menu to victory.

1

Pick a level

Start the next level from the title screen, or revisit a cleared one for a 3-star run.

2

Rotate the pipes

Tap a tile to rotate it. Build a clean, connected path from the bolt to every home on the board.

3

Bring it home

Beat the timer to clear the level, earn gems, and unlock the next puzzle.

FAQ

Quick answers to the things players ask most.

Is Bolt to Home free to play?

Yes. All 200 levels are free. We support development with optional rewarded ads — watch one for extra time, a free Clear Box, or bonus gems. You never have to.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Every level, gem, star, and saved score works offline. Ads only show when you're connected and never block your progress.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up, ever. Your progress is stored on your device.

What devices are supported?

Android 8.0+ and recent iOS, on phones and tablets in portrait mode.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Email brevabeestudios@gmail.com with your device model and OS version. See the Support section above.

How is my data handled?

The game itself doesn't collect personal information. Our advertising SDK collects device identifiers and approximate (IP-based) location to serve ads. Full details in the privacy policy.
